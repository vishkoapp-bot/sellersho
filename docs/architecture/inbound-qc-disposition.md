# Conserved inbound QC disposition (INV-04)

`RecordQCPass` and `CompleteQC` consume exact received quarantine allocations into `qc_accepted` and `damaged`. Neither state is sellable. The [INV-09 checkpoint](exact-inbound-putaway.md) now creates an immutable task and isolated source for each positive `passed_delta`; ConfirmPutaway alone credits bin sellable and storage coverage. QC never repeats the supplier debit. `InboundFullReceiptLifecycleEnabled` remains false until the seven retained downstream branches have been integrated and executed without skips; it is not evidence that this broader acceptance is complete.

Both existing HTTP QC operations accept optional `receiptLineId`; the warehouse queue and current QC UI always supply it. It must identify the product's persisted line within the named shipment. Old callers omitting it work only when that shipment/product has exactly one physical receipt line. Multiple SKU lines never merge and an ambiguous, empty, foreign or unknown selector fails without effects. Supplier/destination/variant identity comes from immutable `inbound_inventory_lines`, never the product's current source.

Passed and failed quantities are **cumulative per receipt line**, including previous decisions. `RecordQCPass(quantity)` means cumulative passed `quantity`, failed zero, shortage zero. `CompleteQC` carries cumulative passed/failed plus the shortage snapshot and reason. The database business identity is `(receipt_line_id, cumulative_disposed)`, where cumulative disposed is passed plus failed. Counts must be nonnegative, fit PostgreSQL integer bounds, individually preserve prior passed/failed totals, and total no more than physically received. A new event must increase total disposed. It consumes only its positive passed/failed deltas from the exact `inbound-line:<id>` quarantine balance. That balance must equal received minus the previously disposed total; unexplained differences require reconciliation.

For example, receive five, pass three: event total three moves three to acceptance and leaves two quarantined. Submitting cumulative passed three / failed two creates event total five and moves only the two remaining units to damaged. After receiving two then five, later cumulative decisions similarly consume only their new deltas. Earlier exact payloads remain acknowledgable after subsequent receipts and dispositions. A known total with a different partition or shortage conflicts; a lower unknown total and regressing passed/failed counters conflict. Both endpoints share these identities. An all-pass opposite-endpoint retry acknowledges the original result; `RecordQCPass` has no reason, so that replay does not replace the original stored reason. When both requests specify reasons, a changed stored nonempty reason conflicts. Actor changes on retry do not rewrite the original actor.

Shortage is audit evidence, never inventory. The existing API requires a positive passed/failed disposition and continues to reject shortage-only requests. Its shortage snapshot cannot exceed declared minus received at that event. Later counted arrivals may reduce the snapshot without altering older immutable reports or manufacturing stock. A positive new shortage creates a discrepancy; exact event replay creates none. Shortage reports do not cancel transit or prove a physical loss. Any final loss/return reconciliation requires its own explicit protocol.

The QC queue reports exact line/SKU identities, cumulative passed/failed counts and remaining quantity. It retains undisposed units after partial QC. Historical ASN/carton receipts lacking allocation metadata remain visible as reconciliation exceptions, with QC mutation disabled. Preexisting QC results without matching disposition evidence also require reconciliation. Migration `202611140008` adds append-only event metadata and the non-sellable status only; it neither backfills history nor subtracts historical damage from current supplier stock. Event update/delete is rejected and business identity is unique in PostgreSQL. Down migration refuses any persisted disposition or accepted stock.

The caller owns one bounded transaction. It acquires supplier, all products, all SKUs, existing shipment/header/item work guards, then the entire physical balance union in bytewise ID order through the INV-02/CORE-01 helpers. Dispositions are subordinate append-only event metadata, serialized by the existing work guard; they introduce no independent late guard or foreign-key acquisition. Stock movements, QC result, discrepancy and immutable event commit together. No provider/network call occurs in the transaction. Existing capture, sellable projection and supplier-return selection require `status='sellable'`; acceptance and damaged/undisposed receipt units cannot satisfy those promises.

The mounted UI presents cumulative quantities with prior decisions as defaults after refresh. Successful acknowledgment clears its submitted form; ambiguous/malformed responses retain the exact cumulative payload for retry. A request that succeeded before its acknowledgment was lost therefore replays the same immutable event instead of adding its quantities again.
